About Silly Sprout Garden

Silly Sprout Garden is a cosy garden game where every seed grows into a funny creature-plant, like a Cactus Penguin, a Banana Shark or a Lettuce Lamb. Plant, water, harvest, sell and collect all 34.

Seeds come in six rarities, and weather can bring Golden, Rainbow and Glowing mutations. A Daily Gift grows each day up to an Epic seed on Day 7.

3 quick tips

  1. Water every sprout, more than once. Each splash cuts the grow time.
  2. Keep every patch busy, and buy more patches early.
  3. Save rare seeds for special weather to get mutations.

How to play

Tap an empty patch and pick a seed packet to plant. Keep tapping empty patches to plant more.

Tap growing plants to water them. Each splash makes them grow faster!

When a creature-plant wiggles and sparkles, tap it to harvest. It joins your Collection Book.

Sell creatures in the basket for coins, then buy rarer seed packets in the Shop.

Weather changes every few minutes. Plants that finish in Sunshine may turn Golden, in a Rainbow may turn Rainbow, and under the Moon may Glow. Rain splashes everyone for you!

Do quests, collect your daily gift, grow your garden and decorate it. Plants keep growing while you are away.
